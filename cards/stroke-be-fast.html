    Warning signs card

    Stroke: BE FAST

    Spot a stroke. Act fast.

    BE FAST: call 112 or 108 now

    • Balance: sudden loss of balance or dizziness
    • Eyes: sudden loss of vision or double vision
    • Face: one side of the face droops
    • Arm: weakness, or one arm drifts down
    • Speech: slurred, muddled, or can't speak or understand
    • Time: call now, and note the time the symptoms started

    While you wait

    • Don't give food, drink or tablets
    • If they are drowsy, lay them on their side
    • Even if the symptoms go away, they still need hospital today

    Emergency: 112  ·  Ambulance: 108

    Based on NHS stroke guidance and the BE FAST stroke signs. General information, not personal medical advice. Checked November 2026.
