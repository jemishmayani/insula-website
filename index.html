Your nervous system works like a message network. Choose a part to see what it does, or send a signal and follow it.

The brain: where decisions are made

It plans movement, makes sense of what you feel, and controls speech, memory and emotion. Different areas do different jobs, which is why the symptoms of a brain problem depend on where it is.

The spinal cord: the main cable

A bundle of nerve fibres about the width of a finger, protected inside a channel in your spine. It carries messages between the brain and the body in both directions.

Nerves: the branches

Nerves leave the spinal cord between the bones of the spine and reach every part of the body, carrying commands out to muscles and feeling back to the brain.

A problem anywhere along this path shows up where the signal was heading. That's why something pressing on a nerve in your back can cause pain in your foot.

Why your back can hurt your leg

HealthyBulgeOn the nerve

Healthy disc

The soft centre sits safely inside its tough outer ring. The nerve beside it has plenty of room.

A bulge

The centre pushes the outer ring outward. Bulges like this are common on scans and often cause no pain at all.

Pressing on the nerve

Now the bulge touches the nerve. Pain, tingling or numbness travels along it into the leg. This is sciatica, and most people improve without surgery.

The island inside your brain

Deep inside each side of the brain, hidden under folds of tissue, is a region called the insula, Latin for island. The folds that cover it are called the opercula, which means lids.

The insula helps you sense what is happening inside your own body: your heartbeat, hunger, breathing, even disgust. Hidden, deep and quietly important, it is where this site gets its name.

Warning signs that can't wait

  • A sudden, severe headache that peaks within seconds
  • Sudden weakness, numbness or drooping on one side of the face or body
  • Numbness around the groin, or new bladder or bowel problems

Call 112 or go to your nearest emergency department. Don't wait for a clinic appointment or a reply to an email.
