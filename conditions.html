Conditions

Plain-language guides to common brain and spine conditions: what they are, how they are diagnosed, and the treatment options.

Brain

Brain

Brain tumours

An abnormal growth of cells in or around the brain. Many are not cancer.

Read more Brain

Brain aneurysm

A small bulge in a weak spot of a blood vessel in the brain. Most never burst.

Read more Brain

Hydrocephalus

A build-up of fluid inside the brain that raises pressure. It can affect any age.

Read more Brain

Head injury

From a mild concussion to bleeding inside the skull. Knowing the warning signs matters.

Read more Brain

Trigeminal neuralgia

Sudden, severe, electric-shock-like pain in one side of the face.

Read more Brain

Stroke

A blocked or burst blood vessel in the brain. Every minute counts.

Read more Brain

Pituitary tumour

A usually benign growth of the hormone gland below the brain. It can affect vision and hormones.

Read more Brain

Neurocysticercosis

Cysts of the pork tapeworm in the brain. A common cause of seizures in India, and treatable.

Read more Brain

Chiari malformation

The lowest part of the brain sits too low, blocking fluid flow at the base of the skull.

Read more Brain

Parkinson's disease and DBS

Tremor, stiffness and slowness, and how deep brain stimulation can help.

Read more

Spine

Spine

Slipped disc and sciatica

A disc pressing on a nerve, often causing leg pain, tingling or numbness.

Read more Spine

Lumbar canal stenosis

Narrowing of the spinal canal in the lower back, causing leg pain when walking.

Read more Spine

Cervical myelopathy

Pressure on the spinal cord in the neck, causing clumsy hands and unsteady walking.

Read more Spine

Cauda equina syndrome

A spinal emergency where nerves controlling the bladder and bowel are squeezed.

Read more Spine

Spinal tuberculosis

A TB infection of the spine. Common in India, and treatable with medicines.

Read more Spine

Cervical radiculopathy

A pinched nerve in the neck, causing pain, numbness or weakness down one arm.

Read more Spine

Spondylolisthesis

One vertebra slipping forward on the one below, which can squeeze the nerves.

Read more Spine

Osteoporotic spine fracture

A vertebra weakened by thin bones collapses, often after a minor fall or even a cough.

Read more
