Not for emergencies

  • A sudden, severe headache
  • Sudden weakness, numbness, or difficulty speaking
  • Numbness around the groin or new bladder or bowel problems
  • Confusion or drowsiness after a head injury

Email and social media messages are not monitored for emergencies. Call 112 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Before you write

Contacting me does not create a doctor-patient relationship, and I can't give advice about your personal medical situation by email. For that, please see a doctor who can examine you. See the medical disclaimer and privacy policy.
